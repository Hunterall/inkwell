# Story Bible To Screen

Story workspace: Chapters, a story bible for characters and world, and an AI that drafts, expands and rewrites in a chosen style. This covers Sudowrite and QuillBot together, since rewrite and paraphrase are really one feature.

One-click story to script: Convert prose into screenplay format with scene breakdowns. This is the bridge between writers and filmmakers, and it’s the part none of the four does well from end to end.

Storyboard and pitch deck export: Scene images from an image-generation API and a PDF deck of synopsis, characters and moodboard.
